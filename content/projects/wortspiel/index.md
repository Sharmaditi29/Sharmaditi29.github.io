LingoGarden is a small German study companion for daily practice. It now focuses on Goethe-based A1, A2, and B1 vocabulary, then layers flashcards, quick concept reminders, sentence prompts, and simple quizzes so review feels active and manageable.

The app is designed for personal use, but the interface is open and approachable enough for anyone else who wants a lightweight browser-based German practice tool.
