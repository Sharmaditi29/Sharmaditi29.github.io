Data source

Built from a small curated summary of the UKE/IMS xenium-cgn analysis workflow and the associated spatial kidney disease paper. The raw spatial datasets are listed by the authors as GEO GSE294965 and snRNA-seq GSE303481.

Prototype scope

The embedded numbers are relative display indices, not a substitute for full-resolution Xenium coordinates. The structure is ready for a future export of cell centroids, annotations, and pathway scores.
